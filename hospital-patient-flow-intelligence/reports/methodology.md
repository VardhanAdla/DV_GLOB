## 2. Queuing Simulation & Arrival Dynamics

Patient arrivals were simulated using empirical healthcare probability distributions:
* **Outpatient Clinic Rhythms:** Outpatient presentations follow a bi-modal probability density function peaking at 09:00–12:00 (morning walk-ins and scheduled appointments) and 16:00–18:00 (afternoon appointments).
* **Emergency Department Rhythms:** Emergency presentations operate 24 hours a day with sustained volume in the late evening and early night shifts (18:00–01:00) and elevated trauma cases on weekends.
* **Queuing Delays:** Service queues at each station (Registration, Triage, Doctor, Diagnostics, Treatment, Pharmacy) were modeled using an $M/M/c$ queuing formulation where service wait times increase exponentially as demand approaches staff service capacity:
  $$W_q \approx \frac{\rho^{\sqrt{2(c+1)}-1}}{c(1-\rho)} \cdot \frac{1}{\mu}$$
  where $\rho = \frac{\lambda}{c \mu}$, $\lambda$ is hourly arrival rate, $c$ is active staff on duty, and $\mu$ is clinician consultation speed.

## 3. Data Cleansing & Transformation Rules

To reflect authentic healthcare EHR conditions, 30,450 raw records were subjected to a rigorous, auditable cleaning pipeline implemented in `src/clean_hospital_data.py`:
1. **Deduplication:** 450 duplicate encounters generated by simulated multi-terminal network retries were eliminated on primary key `Visit_ID`.
2. **Standardization:** Department terminology (`EMERGENCY`, `Emerg`, `ER` $\to$ `Emergency`; `Paediatrics` $\to$ `Pediatrics`) and gender strings (`F`, `female` $\to$ `Female`; `M`, `male` $\to$ `Male`) were mapped to canonical domains.
3. **Imputation:** Missing age groups were imputed based on clinical department context (Pediatrics $\to$ 0-17; adult OPD $\to$ 18-35). Missing arrival modes were imputed based on triage classification (Emergency $\to$ Ambulance; OPD $\to$ Walk-in).
4. **Chronological Sequence Repair:** Timestamp inversions where physician consultation start was logged prior to clinical triage completion were re-anchored to:
   $$\text{Doctor Consultation Start} = \text{Triage End Time} + \text{Doctor Wait Time}$$
5. **Negative Value Rectification:** Negative waiting times resulting from unsynchronized workstation clocks were replaced by median queue durations stratified by clinical priority tier (Critical: 3m, High: 14m, Medium: 32m, Low: 48m).
6. **Typo Winsorization:** Extreme typographical entry values (e.g. 9999 minutes) were capped at the 99th percentile boundary (120 minutes).

## 4. Analytical Metric Engineering

### 4.1 Bottleneck Score Formulation
The composite Bottleneck Score (0 to 100) measures operational friction without asserting medical diagnostic authority:
$$\text{Bottleneck Score} = 0.40 \cdot \min\left(100, \frac{W_{\text{doctor}}}{75} \cdot 100\right) + 0.35 \cdot (\text{Bed Occupancy Rate} \cdot 100) + 0.25 \cdot \min\left(100, \frac{\text{Capacity}}{\text{Staff} \times 4} \cdot 50\right)$$

* **Component Weights:**
  * **40% Doctor Wait Time:** Primary bottleneck reflecting clinical access delays.
  * **35% Bed Occupancy Rate:** Measures physical capacity exhaustion.
  * **25% Staff Stretch Ratio:** Measures clinical staffing burden.

## 5. Tableau Visual Architecture

The visual analytics layer was constructed across 14 specialized worksheets unified into 4 purpose-built dashboards:
* **Dashboard 1 (Executive Flow Overview):** Provides the daily and hourly volume baseline, department volume rankings, and triage priority distribution.
* **Dashboard 2 (Department Bottleneck Analysis):** Features a 4-quadrant Volume vs. Wait scatter plot and a 12x24 department-hour congestion heatmap.
* **Dashboard 3 (Patient Journey & Waiting Time):** Visualizes the multi-stage funnel progression and cumulative queue delay breakdown.
* **Dashboard 4 (Operational Intelligence):** Delivers parameter-driven dynamic trend exploration and statistical anomaly tracking.
